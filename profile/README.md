# Arkin Laboratory

Computational and experimental biology at [UC Berkeley](https://bioeng.berkeley.edu/) and [Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory](https://www.lbl.gov/).

We develop methods and tools for microbial systems biology, synthetic biology, and predictive biology. We lead and contribute to large collaborative research programs including [KBase](https://www.kbase.us/), [ENIGMA](https://enigma.lbl.gov/), and the [Phage Foundry](https://www.jbei.org/).
