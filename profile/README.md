# Arkin Laboratory for Systems and Synthetic Biology

The Arkin Laboratory develops methods and tools for microbial systems biology, synthetic biology, and predictive biology at UC Berkeley and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, led by Adam P. Arkin (Dean A. Richard Newton Memorial Professor, Bioengineering, UC Berkeley; Senior Faculty Scientist, Environmental Genomics & Systems Biology Division, LBNL).

We seek to uncover the evolutionary design principles of cellular networks and populations and exploit them for applications in health, the environment, and the circular bioeconomy on Earth and in space. Our work combines comparative functional genomics, quantitative measurement of cellular dynamics, biophysical modeling, and cellular circuit design.

For full lab details, visit [arkinlab.bio](https://arkinlab.bio) or [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=h6JyMQgAAAAJ).

## Genomics Tools and Resources

Interactive tools for functional annotation of bacterial genomes developed by the lab and close collaborators, primarily Morgan Price (LBNL).

Reference: Price & Arkin (2024) "Interactive Tools for Functional Annotation of Bacterial Genomes" [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.04.15.589591v1)

| Tool | URL | What It Does |
|------|-----|-------------|
| PaperBLAST | [papers.genomics.lbl.gov](https://papers.genomics.lbl.gov) | Find papers about a protein or its homologs |
| Curated BLAST | [papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/genomeSearch.cgi](https://papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/genomeSearch.cgi) | Find characterized proteins by function within a genome |
| GapMind (amino acids) | [papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/gapView.cgi?set=aa](https://papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/gapView.cgi?set=aa) | Predict amino acid biosynthesis pathway completeness |
| GapMind (carbon) | [papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/gapView.cgi?set=carbon](https://papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/gapView.cgi?set=carbon) | Predict carbon catabolism pathway completeness |
| SitesBLAST | [papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/sites.cgi](https://papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/sites.cgi) | Find proteins with similar functional sites |
| Sites on a Tree | [papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/treeSites.cgi](https://papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/treeSites.cgi) | Visualize functional sites across a protein family tree |
| Family Search vs. Papers | [papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/hmmSearch.cgi](https://papers.genomics.lbl.gov/cgi-bin/hmmSearch.cgi) | HMM-based search linking protein families to literature |
| Papers vs. PDB | [papers.genomics.lbl.gov/vspdb](https://papers.genomics.lbl.gov/vspdb) | Link literature to protein structures |
| fast.genomics | [fast.genomics.lbl.gov](https://fast.genomics.lbl.gov) | Genome browser for bacteria and archaea |
| Fitness Browser | [fit.genomics.lbl.gov](https://fit.genomics.lbl.gov) | Genome-wide mutant fitness data |
| MicrobesOnline | [microbesonline.org](https://microbesonline.org) | Comparative genomics resource |
| FaSTree | [github.com/morgannprice/fasttree](https://github.com/morgannprice/fasttree) | Fast phylogenetic tree construction (also on KBase) |
| GenoPHI | [github.com/Noonanav](https://github.com/Noonanav) | Phage-host interaction prediction using ML ([bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.11.15.688630v1)) |
